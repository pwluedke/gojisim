Text:
Choose 3 battle cards in your zones in ascending order of rank and [destroy] them. If 3 cards are [destroy] this way, play 1 battle card with the highest rank from your discard pile.

Rulings (Paul, Nov 4, 2026):
- The 3 are your lowest-ranked battle cards; you choose among ties. With fewer than 3 battle
  cards, destroy what's there and play nothing.
- Crystals tokens (rank 1) count, and are banished, not discarded (9.1.4.2).
- "Play" (出現) is a normal play from the discard pile: [enter] triggers, you pick the zone,
  ordinary placement rules.
- Highest rank: printed rank, so Mecha-King Ghidorah (EBP02-068, rank 23) is the highest; you
  choose among ties.
