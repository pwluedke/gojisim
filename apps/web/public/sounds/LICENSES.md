Every file here is CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication, <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>). Each was converted to mono 64 kbps MP3, trimmed, faded out and loudness-normalized with ffmpeg; two are mixes of two CC0 sources.
