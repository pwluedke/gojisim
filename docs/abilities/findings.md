## Ruling questions for Paul

Starter cards first.

1. **Q1 (ESD01-011, ESD01-012):** "When this card is [destroy], place this card on the bottom of your deck instead." Does it also apply when the card is crushed by its master's own monster (11.3) or loses an overload (11.5)? Both count as destroyed (5.12.1.1). Proposed: yes.
2. **Q2 (ESD02-004):** "Discard 1 battle card from your hand: [destroy] ...". Is the discard optional (no discard, no destroy)? With no battle card in hand, does the ability do nothing? Proposed: optional (10.6.3.2.1); nothing happens without a discard.
3. **Q3 (ESD02-003):** "Play 2 rank 4 or lower battle cards with [evolution] from your discard pile in zones adjacent to this card." With only 1 legal card, play 1? May a card go to an adjacent zone that already holds a battle card (11.5 then destroys the old one)? EBP02-028 says so explicitly; this card doesn't. Proposed: play as many as possible (1.3.2), empty zones first, occupied zones allowed.
4. **Q4 (ESD02-005, all [invading] cards):** ESD02-005's reminder says When invading triggers once per zone advanced. Does that hold for every [invading] ability? Then ESD01-002 searches twice and ESD02-004 asks for two discards on an icon 2 invasion. Proposed: yes (12.3.2: the trigger is the monster "advancing from an invasion").
5. **Q5 (ESD01-012):** "[Your Turn] When you play a monster card, you may move this card to an unoccupied zone." Does the zone your own invading monster occupies count as unoccupied? Moving there would crush the card (11.3). Does a Burst play count? Proposed: the monster's zone is not a legal destination (5.11.1.2 by analogy); Burst plays count.
6. **Q6 (ESD01-010 City of Tokyo):** "Your other battle card in zone 8 gains +5000." When City of Tokyo is itself in zone 8, the bonus has no target (one stack per zone). Do both abilities stack to +10,000 when both conditions hold? Proposed: yes, and no target means no effect.
7. **Q7 (ESD02-009 Super-X):** "[awakening-4][enter] If this card is in zone 8, reduce your opponent's [rage] by 1." Must your monster be in zone 4 or beyond when Super-X is played (trigger) or when the ability resolves? Proposed: both, since an inactive ability doesn't trigger (10.3.2) and the condition is checked again on resolution.
8. **Q8 (ESD02-014, ESD02-010):** (a) "Evolve 1 of your battle cards with [evolution]": where does the new card come from? Proposed: search the deck for a card matching that card's Evolution rank and trait, as 12.6.2 says; optional to find (5.8.1.2); then shuffle. (b) Is an evolved card "played" (so [enter] and "played through evolution" trigger, as on ESD02-010)? Proposed: yes, as with the Evolution keyword.
9. **Q9 (F1):** Do `[Your Turn]` and `[Opponent's Turn]` mean "active only during that player's turn", for continuous and automatic abilities alike? Are `[opponent-turn]` and `[opponents-turn]` the same keyword? Proposed: yes to both.
10. **Q10 (F2, ESD01-013 Ginza Annihilated):** A strategy card whose text is its own trigger stays active in the strategy zone until the next start phase (7.2.3), and does nothing on placement. Proposed: yes. With `[Your Turn]`, Ginza then only matters for the rest of the turn it is activated.

Other sets (M12, but they shape the M4b format):

11. **Q11 (EBP04-089 Inherited Life):** How are "[rage] placed under this card" represented, given that rage cards aren't cards (3.4.2.1)? Proposed: a counter on the card, which "the Nth card placed under" reads.
12. **Q12 (F11):** Treat the typos as their obvious corrections ("Godzilla Earth", "to your hand") through a text-override file, as with trait spellings (decision 53)?
13. **Q13 (F9):** Should the deck validator let "any number of this card" cards exceed 4 copies (1.3.1)? Proposed: yes; a small change to `validateDeck`, in M4b or M8.
14. **Q14 (EBP01-038, EBP01-066, EBP01-077, EBP02-027):** Does "moves as though it were countered" count as being countered, for triggers such as "when this card is countered" (EBP04-022) or "when you successfully counter" (EBP01-043)? Proposed: no; only the move happens.

## The 10 hardest starter cards to implement

1. **ESD02-014 The Legend of Infant Island:** an Evolve instruction with no named result (Q8). It needs the target's Evolution rank and trait, a hidden deck search that may find nothing, stacking (the card underneath loses its abilities, 3.2.3.1), a shuffle, and then the new card's [enter].
2. **ESD02-007 Mothra (Larva) (1992)** (and ESD02-008 Battra (Larva), which works the same way): an optional main phase trigger, a deck search by trait and maximum rank, playing onto the stack, a shuffle, and "played through evolution" recorded for ESD02-010. Only the top card's counter power counts afterwards.
3. **ESD02-003 Godzilla (1992):** [enter] on a monster, 2 targets from the discard pile, adjacency from the monster's position (4.4.5.2), different zones (5.11.1.3), never the monster's own zone (5.11.1.1). Each played card's own [enter] chains, e.g. Super-X.
4. **ESD02-004 Godzilla (1994):** an undefined cost form with a hidden-hand choice (Q2), a rank filter taken from the discarded card's last known information (10.10), "all" targets, and once-per-advance triggering (Q4).
5. **ESD01-007 Godzilla (2023)** (and the other Burst cards, ESD01-005 and ESD01-006): Burst changes 8.5 play legality, then a timed trigger at the next end phase discards the card even if it isn't on top (12.5.2.1). The card underneath becomes active again without "entering" (12.5.3). On top of that comes a same-column destroy.
6. **ESD01-012 Godzilla (2023):** a turn-gated trigger on playing a monster card, an optional move to an unoccupied zone (Q5), a zone condition and a replacement effect, all on one battle card.
7. **ESD01-013 Ginza Annihilated:** a strategy card whose text is its own trigger (F2, Q10). It fires on every rage increase, whatever the source (the gain rage action, a monster play, effects), and makes a targeted destroy choice each time.
8. **ESD01-011 Godzilla (2023):** the first replacement effect (10.9): its own event type, its interaction with rule-action destruction (Q1), and no Revenge because the card never reaches the discard pile.
9. **ESD01-010 City of Tokyo:** continuous bonuses applied to a different card, chosen by zone and "other". Each is gated by a condition that changes during play (rage, Awakening) and is read at 7.4.2.
10. **ESD01-014 Godzilla Emerges:** a conditional search by exact name, playing from the deck to a chosen zone without the 8.2.1 rank limit (5.11.1), the played card's [enter] (ESD01-011 then reduces rage), and a shuffle.

## Decisions made in this survey

- Scope uses one card per `deckCardNumber`, its lowest-id base print, with errata applied through `getCardText`. Rage and Token cards are excluded; Tokens get their own table.
- A clause counts as classified when at least one effect matches it, or a choice or branch that sets up the next clause. Parenthesized text never counts as unclassified; text beyond the standard keyword reminders is listed under "Rules clarifications in parentheses".
- Strategy cards get an implicit `strategy card placed (10.1.1.1.2)` trigger on their first ability.
- Keywords add their implied parts: Awakening adds a zone condition, Burst a duration, Evolution a main phase trigger, Revenge a destroyed trigger, and the turn keywords a whose-turn condition.
- Where the starter reminder text and a keyword's rule differ (ESD02-007, ESD02-008), the rule (12.6.2) governs.
- `survey.md` and `survey.json` are generated by `pnpm survey` and are in `.prettierignore`. This file is hand-written.
